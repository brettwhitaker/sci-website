About

Hi, I'm Brett.

I've been writing for as long as I can remember. I started writing professionally in college as a communication specialist for the Office of Communication Studies at Indiana University Indianapolis (IUPUI), where encouragement from my professors turned a lifelong habit into a career.

I grew up in Indianapolis and moved to Northeast Florida after college to build a life with my family. It's where my career began, and where Strategic Creative Initiatives calls home.

Writing is how I find the exact words for what I mean, and for the feeling I want someone else to have. Good writing can paint a picture and give a reader the same feeling as a great lived experience. Today I help businesses do that for their own customers: I act as their voice, helping them say exactly what they mean.

My favorite thing to write? Sports. It brings together everything I love: storytelling, competition and a good statistical deep dive.
